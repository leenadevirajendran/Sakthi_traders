SECTOR 01

Automotive & Transportation

Keeping commercial logistics fleets, interstate truck carriers, passenger bus operators, and intra-city logistics moving with maximum uptime and fuel efficiency.

In the transport industry, tyre casing life and battery cranking dependability directly dictate operating margins. We supply multi-axle freight fleets and corporate logistics operators with high-mileage Apollo and JK radial tyres, alongside high-cold-cranking Amaron commercial batteries and API CK-4 certified heavy-duty diesel engine oils from Castrol and MAK.

Supplied Materials: Commercial vehicle radial tyres (295/80 R22.5, 10.00 R20) • Amaron commercial starting batteries (100–180Ah) • MAK / Castrol heavy diesel engine oils (15W-40) • Wheel bearing greases
SECTOR 02

Manufacturing & Industrial

Powering precision machine tool builders, heavy fabrication yards, casting foundries, and automated manufacturing plants across Tamil Nadu and southern India.

Heavy machinery operates under continuous thermal and mechanical stress. We provide hydraulic fluids formulated with shear-stable viscosity improvers from Shell and MAK, extreme-pressure lithium and moly greases for heavy roll stands, structural steel plates and beams for factory expansions, and lint-free cotton yarn waste for clean machine maintenance.

Supplied Materials: Anti-wear hydraulic fluids (ISO VG 32/46/68) • Industrial gear oils (ISO VG 220–460) • EP Lithium and Moly greases • 100% white cotton wiping rags • Structural steel sections
SECTOR 03

Construction & Infrastructure

Providing foundational strength to highway corridors, industrial sheds, commercial complexes, metro rail infrastructure, and bridges across southern India.

Civil infrastructure requires verifiable metallurgical and compressive strength. We supply primary integrated Suryadev Fe 550D TMT steel rebars accompanied by mill heat test certificates, fresh bulk dispatches of Zuari Grade 53 OPC and PPC cement, and machine-crushed granite blue metal aggregates directly to project batching plants.

Supplied Materials: Suryadev Fe 550D TMT bars (8mm–32mm) • Zuari Grade 53 OPC & PPC bulk cement • Graded blue metal aggregates (10mm, 20mm, 40mm) • Manufactured Sand (M-Sand / P-Sand)
SECTOR 04

Textile Industry

Supporting spinning mills, modern shuttleless weaving units, processing houses, and garment export enterprises with essential maintenance and cleaning supplies.

Textile machinery requires absolute cleanliness and uninterrupted operation. We supply premium pure cotton yarn waste for loom maintenance, specialized high-speed non-staining spindle oils, backup battery arrays for continuous automation power, and maintenance greases that resist lint accumulation.

Supplied Materials: Pure virgin white cotton yarn waste • Soft lint-free wiping rags • Industrial spindle oils • Standby tubular UPS battery arrays • Textile machinery maintenance greases
