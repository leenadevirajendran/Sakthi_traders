CATEGORY 04 • BS-VI EMISSION FLUID & SCR AFTERTREATMENT

Gulf AdBlue® Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) – ISO 22241

Direct factory distribution of certified genuine Gulf AdBlue® Aqueous Urea Solution 32 (AUS 32) formulated to maintain peak SCR catalytic conversion efficiency and full BS-VI emission compliance across commercial diesel transport fleets.

Under Bharat Stage VI (BS-VI) norms, all medium and heavy diesel commercial vehicles—including trucks, multi-axle tippers, and prime movers from Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, BharatBenz, Eicher, Volvo, and Scania—utilize Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) technology. Injected directly into the exhaust stream upstream of the SCR catalyst, AdBlue thermally decomposes to convert harmful nitrogen oxides (NOx) into harmless atmospheric nitrogen gas (N2) and pure water vapor (H2O), cutting toxic tailpipe emissions by over 90%.

⚠️ Critical Fleet Advisory: Why Certified Genuine AdBlue® is Non-Negotiable

Using non-certified or spurious DEF (such as dissolved agricultural fertilizer urea or unpurified water) introduces lethal mineral ions (calcium, iron, copper, zinc) and excessive biuret. Contaminated urea permanently poisons the precious-metal SCR catalyst, clogs dosing injector nozzles, and instantly triggers the vehicle ECU's mandatory OBD-II Torque Derate Mode—reducing engine power by 40–50% and capping road speed to 20 km/h. Replacing a poisoned SCR converter and dosing unit costs between ₹1,50,000 and ₹3,50,000 per truck! Certified Gulf AdBlue® guarantees zero derate breakdowns, maximum fuel economy, and full OEM warranty compliance.
